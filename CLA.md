## How to sign

Add your GitHub username to `.github/cla-signatures.txt`, one per line, in the
same pull request as your first contribution.

**The check is a human one.** This repository runs no CI (ADR-036), so nothing
verifies the signature automatically and `main` has no required status checks.
The maintainer reads the file when reviewing your first pull request. If CI
returns, a `cla` check is the natural thing to register — until then, do not
expect an automated verdict on your PR.

Signing once covers all your future contributions.
